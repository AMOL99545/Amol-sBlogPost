Who I am

A future engineer thinking
out loud from Kathmandu

I'm Amol — a fourth-year Civil Engineering student at IOE Pulchowk Campus, Nepal's most competitive engineering school. I started this blog because I couldn't find honest, on-the-ground writing about what it's like to study and think about infrastructure in Nepal — a country that has had to rebuild itself multiple times after devastating earthquakes.

Here I share opinions on structural design, write about my projects, document campus life, and push back on engineering choices I think we get wrong. I believe engineers should communicate their ideas clearly — this blog is where I practise that.
